Questions people ask

Will this make me money?

Nobody can promise that, and Open Practice never does. This workshop helps you make work that is worth paying for and keep the evidence. Whether anyone buys depends on your market, your outreach and your work.

Which build should I start with?

Build 01, unless you already have a buyer who asked for something specific. An offer sheet tells you which of the other builds is worth your time.

Can I use these files commercially?

Yes. The packs and kits are CC BY 4.0: use, change and share them with credit to Open Practice. Your filled-in work is yours.
