ChatGPT vs Google Gemini

ChatGPT vs Gemini, tested your way: a 20-minute side-by-side with three real prompts and a scoring rubric. Decide with evidence, not hype.

What is actually different

  • Gemini connects closely to Google Search and Workspace; ChatGPT has a large ecosystem of custom GPTs and integrations.
  • Both offer free plans with usage limits that change; image and file features vary by plan.
  • Gemini offers a free developer key in Google AI Studio; ChatGPT’s API is paid.

Plans and features change often. Checked October 2026 — confirm on each official site.

The 20-minute test

Run the same three tasks on both. Use your own real material where you can.

  1. Summarise a public 3-page report in 5 bullets with quotes.
  2. Draft a 120-word customer apology for a late delivery.
  3. Explain compound interest to a 14-year-old with a local example.

Quick decision

Choose ChatGPT if you already live in Microsoft/OpenAI tools or want custom GPTs.

Choose Google Gemini if you use Google Docs, Gmail and Drive, or want a free API key.
